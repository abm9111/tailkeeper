# The acl_test run is among them: a posture map dropped from the request, the
# subject screen skipped, another source's results failing the call, a reply
# shown without the resolver's mask, and an unevaluable test exiting 9.
# scripts/mutate.go says what the format is and what the three outcomes mean. The list
# holds one mutant for each class of defect the tests of the subject resolver, the
# pseudonym substitution, the failure-line grammar, the verdicts of acl_validate and the
# comparison of acl_diff were written to catch.
